# Snap-Fit Design Plan

Last updated: 2026-06-29

## Corrected Direction

Each physical icon part should be its own wall-mounted fixture:

- A small hidden backplate is generated for every printed icon part.
- Each backplate has two small through-holes so it can be pinned to the wall and mechanically oriented.
- The visible translucent front piece is a hollow cap that snaps over that specific backplate.
- The full icon is assembled on the wall by placing each part/backplate pair in the icon layout.

This is the only supported mechanical direction for the current prototype.

## First Geometry

Current first-pass fixture model:

- Front cap depth: `8.0 mm`
- Front cap side wall thickness: `1.0 mm`
- Front cap visible face thickness: `1.2 mm`
- Front cap cavity depth: `6.8 mm`
- Backplate thickness: `3.0 mm`
- Backplate XY fit clearance: `0.25 mm`
- Backplate Z clearance inside the cap: `0.3 mm`
- Mounting holes: two `1.6 mm` through-holes per backplate
- Mounting hole edge clearance: `1.0 mm`
- Minimum mounting hole spacing: `4.0 mm`

The backplate footprint is generated by insetting the visible icon-part footprint by the front wall thickness plus fit clearance. The front cap cavity is generated by insetting the visible footprint by the front wall thickness. This creates a simple press-fit cap around the small hidden backplate.

## Next Validation

1. Open the corrected Platform fixture STEP in PrusaSlicer and confirm separate bodies import cleanly.
2. Print one or two representative part/backplate pairs before printing the full Platform kit.
3. Test pin-hole usability on a representative wall/cubicle material.
4. Test cap insertion/removal and record whether `0.25 mm` XY clearance is too tight, loose, or acceptable.
5. Tune front wall thickness, fit clearance, and backplate thickness before applying the fixture kit to all icons.

Do not lock the cap clearance or backplate hole parameters until a physical print has been handled.
