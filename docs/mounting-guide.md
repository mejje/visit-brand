# Mounting Guide

How to attach icon backplates to a wall or cubicle.

**Status:** BLOCKED / EXTERNAL — the mounting method has not been approved for workplace use. Confirm with facilities before pinning anything to office property. This guide lists the options under consideration.

## How mounting works

Every icon part has its own small backplate with two through-holes (1.6 mm by default). The hollow front cap snaps over the backplate and hides it completely. Pins or fasteners hold only the small hidden backplates.

Because each part mounts independently, you can place parts directly on the wall with no full-size backing plate.

## Fastener options

| Option | Holds well on | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Push pins | Fabric-covered cubicle panels | Least invasive; check pin length vs panel thickness |
| Small nails | Drywall, cork boards | Leaves holes; may be disallowed on office walls |
| Removable adhesive strips | Smooth painted walls, glass | No holes; surface must be clean; weight limits apply |
| Magnetic plates (premium variant) | Steel surfaces, whiteboards | Not yet implemented in generated kits |
| Over-wall hooks | Cubicle panel tops | Only for hanging layouts, not part backplates |

## Before you mount

1. Confirm which fasteners are allowed where you work.
2. Check your panel type: fabric panels take pins; painted drywall needs adhesive or nails; glass needs adhesive only.
3. Test one backplate first:
   - mount it,
   - snap its cap on,
   - leave it overnight,
   - confirm it holds and the cap stays seated.

## Mounting steps

1. Lay out or dry-fit the icon shape (see `assembly-guide.md`).
2. For each part, mark the two hole positions through the backplate (or hold the plate and pin through it).
3. Fasten the backplate with both holes. Two holes prevent rotation.
4. Snap the front cap on. If the cap hides the fasteners, you are done — the mounting hardware is invisible.

## Removal

- Remove caps by pulling them straight off the backplate.
- Remove pins/nails carefully; adhesive strips should be pulled slowly by the tab.
- Backplates are reusable; if a strip loses tack, replace the adhesive rather than the plate.

## Safety and policy notes

- Never exceed the weight rating of adhesive strips; each backplate plus cap is light, but a large icon is many pieces.
- Do not pin into electrical runs, plumbing, or structural glass.
- Some offices prohibit wall penetrations entirely; use adhesive or a freestanding display instead.
- Remove icons before office moves or deep cleaning of panels.
